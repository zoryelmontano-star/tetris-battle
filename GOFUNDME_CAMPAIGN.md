## Campaign Title

**Help Build KO Blocks: A Multiplayer Social Arcade Made for Quick, Competitive Fun**

## Short Pitch

KO Blocks started as a small multiplayer block-battle prototype. The bigger vision is a browser-first social arcade where players can compete, spectate, join public arenas, build persistent profiles, and eventually move between multiple original games from one shared hub.

The first three planned experiences are:

- **KO Blocks** — fast multiplayer block-clearing battles
- **Mind Clash** — competitive trivia with a continuously expanding, no-repeat question system
- **Type Rush** — typing races and skill-building challenges for solo and multiplayer play

This fundraiser will help turn the working prototype into a polished, secure, scalable multiplayer platform.

## Full Campaign Story

### From one playable prototype to a shared arcade universe

I started building a multiplayer block-battle game because I wanted something quick, competitive, social, and easy to play from almost any device.

What began as a simple experiment grew into a much bigger idea.

Instead of creating separate games that never connect to each other, the goal is to build a shared social arcade: one place where players can create a profile, join public or private rooms, compete with friends or strangers, watch live matches, earn ranks and achievements, and move between different games without starting over each time.

The current prototype already explores browser-based gameplay, mobile controls, real-time multiplayer rooms, matchmaking, live opponent boards, battle statistics, rankings, spectator-style layouts, and installable PWA support.

The next step is turning that prototype into a reliable product.

### Game 1: KO Blocks

KO Blocks is the flagship competitive block-battle game.

Planned modes include:

- Solo Challenge
- 1v1 Battle
- Battle Arena for 2 to 6 players
- Tutorial Mode for beginners
- Public and invite-only arenas
- Browse Arenas with live player counts and room status
- Request to Join
- Queue for Next Round
- Watch Live spectator mode
- spectator reactions and virtual arcade gifts
- rankings, streaks, achievements, and progression

The goal is to keep competitive outcomes skill-based and avoid pay-to-win mechanics.

### Game 2: Mind Clash

Mind Clash is a competitive quiz game for solo play, 1v1, and matches of up to four players.

Players will compete through Easy, Medium, and Hard rounds across categories such as:

Philippines, K-Drama, history, world knowledge, geography, science, technology, movies, music, literature, mythology, food, sports, and general knowledge.

A major feature is a persistent no-repeat question system. Each approved question will receive a unique ID, and the platform will track which questions a player has already seen. The question bank will be designed to grow continuously through a controlled workflow:

**New → Verify → Approved → Live**

### Game 3: Type Rush

Type Rush is a typing game designed to be both fun and useful.

Modes will include solo practice, 1v1 typing races, multiplayer arenas, speed runs, accuracy challenges, endurance, numbers and symbols, and practical typing modes for office, customer support, executive-assistant, and legal work.

Players will be able to track words per minute, accuracy, consistency, and improvement over time.

### The bigger vision

The long-term goal is a shared arcade platform with:

- one player identity across games
- avatars and profiles
- friends and parties
- achievements and badges
- public and private rooms
- live spectators
- tournaments and seasonal events
- cosmetics that do not create competitive advantages
- community challenges
- eventually, lightweight creator tools for custom trivia packs, typing challenges, tournament formats, and room presets

The project is intended to remain easy to enter. A new player should be able to start playing within seconds, without being forced through a complicated registration process.

## FAQ

### Is KO Blocks already finished?

No. A working prototype exists, but the fundraiser is intended to help turn it into a polished, original, production-ready game and then build the shared arcade around it.

### Is this an investment?

No. Donations do not provide equity, ownership, revenue share, profit participation, or investment returns.

### Will the games be pay-to-win?

The product direction is to keep competitive outcomes based on skill, speed, knowledge, and strategy. Monetization may eventually include cosmetics, supporter features, premium convenience features, sponsorships, or optional advertising, but direct competitive advantages are not part of the plan.

### Why build more than one game?

The three games serve different reasons to return while sharing the same multiplayer and social infrastructure:

- KO Blocks: compete
- Mind Clash: learn and compete
- Type Rush: improve and compete

Shared profiles, rooms, friends, achievements, events, and spectator systems reduce the need to rebuild the same platform for every new game.

### Will there be a mobile app?

The project is browser-first and installable as a PWA. Native or packaged mobile distribution is part of a later development stage once the core platform is stable.

### Is the KO Blocks name final?

KO Blocks is currently the working commercial name. Final branding will undergo appropriate trademark and intellectual-property review before commercial launch.
